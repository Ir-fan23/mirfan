Selected Awards & Honors

ICAM Travel Grant (2026)

Awarded by the Institute for Complex Adaptive Matter (ICAM-I2CAM) to support travel and participation in the ICAM Annual Conference in Davis, California. This award recognizes promising research in condensed matter and quantum materials.

3-Minute Thesis Competition Winner (2026)

Winner of the Department of Physics 3-Minute Thesis competition at Florida State University. The award recognizes excellence in communicating complex scientific research to a broad audience in a concise and engaging manner.

APS Distinguished Student Award (2025)

Awarded by the American Physical Society Forum on International Physics and the Forum of Early Career Scientists in recognition of outstanding academic achievement, research contributions, and leadership within the physics community.

Yung Li Wang Award (2025)

Received from the Department of Physics at Florida State University for research contributions toward understanding Topological Kondo Insulators and unconventional superconductivity.

David L. Wilder Award (2024)

Awarded by the Department of Physics at Florida State University in recognition of excellence in research on strongly correlated quantum materials, including Kondo insulators and heavy fermion systems.

Best Poster Award, Rice University Winter School (2025)

Second Prize for the poster presentation titled "Unconventional Superconductivity and the Cooper Pair Formation in CeCoIn5", recognizing excellence in scientific presentation and research communication.

FSU Quantum Conference Travel Award (2025-2026)

Competitive travel support awarded by the FSU Quantum Initiative to facilitate participation in major conferences and promote dissemination of research in quantum materials and condensed matter physics.

Sir Syed Global Scholar Award (2019)

Prestigious award presented by the Sir Syed Education Society of North America recognizing academic excellence and leadership among emerging scholars from India.
