Leadership & Service

President, APS Student Chapter at Florida State University (2025)

As President of the American Physical Society Student Chapter at Florida State University, I coordinated scientific, professional, and outreach activities aimed at strengthening the physics community on campus. I worked closely with faculty, graduate students, and APS leadership to organize events that promoted research communication, career development, and student engagement in physics.

Vice President, APS Student Chapter (2024-2025)

Prior to serving as President, I held the position of Vice President, helping organize chapter activities, professional development workshops, and networking opportunities for physics students. This role provided valuable experience in coordinating large student initiatives and supporting the chapter's long-term growth.

Secretary, APS Student Chapter (2023-2024)

As Secretary, I maintained organizational records, coordinated communication among members, and supported chapter operations. This role served as my introduction to student leadership within the APS community and laid the foundation for future leadership positions.

Treasurer, Physics Graduate Student Association (2024-2025)

As Treasurer of the Physics Graduate Student Association, I managed event budgets, tracked organizational expenditures, and assisted in planning activities that enhanced graduate student engagement within the Department of Physics. This role strengthened my experience in financial management and organizational planning.

Lab Safety Officer, Greene Research Group (2023-Present)

Serving as Lab Safety Officer in the Greene Research Group at the National High Magnetic Field Laboratory, I help maintain compliance with laboratory safety procedures, coordinate training activities, and promote best practices in the safe operation of cryogenic, vacuum, and thin-film deposition systems. This role reflects my commitment to maintaining a safe and productive research environment.

REU Co-Mentor and Undergraduate Research Supervisor

I have mentored undergraduate researchers through Research Experiences for Undergraduates (REU) projects and undergraduate thesis work at the National High Magnetic Field Laboratory. Responsibilities include introducing students to experimental condensed matter physics, training them on laboratory techniques, assisting with data analysis, and guiding scientific communication through posters and presentations.

Finance Lead, Sir Syed Society of North America (2022-2025)

As Finance Lead for the Sir Syed Global Scholar Award program, I was responsible for managing scholarship-related financial operations and supporting strategic planning efforts. This role provided extensive experience in organizational leadership, budgeting, and collaboration with an international network of scholars and alumni.

Application Team Lead, Sir Syed Society of North America (2023-2024)

I led the scholarship application review process, coordinated with evaluation committees, and helped streamline applicant assessment procedures. Through this position, I contributed to identifying and supporting talented students pursuing higher education and research opportunities.

Vice President, Cricket Association at Florida State University

In addition to academic leadership, I served as Vice President of the Cricket Association at Florida State University. This role involved organizing student activities, coordinating competitions, and fostering engagement within the broader university community.
