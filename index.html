About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Physics at Florida State University and a Graduate Research Assistant at the National High Magnetic Field Laboratory. My research focuses on strongly correlated quantum materials,including topological Kondo insulators, heavy-fermion systems, unconventional superconductors, and quantum critical phenomena.I employ planar tunneling spectroscopy, electrical transport,thermodynamic measurements, and high magnetic fields to probeemergent electronic states.

Research Interests

  • Topological Kondo Insulators (SmB₆ and YbB₁₂)
  • Heavy Fermion Superconductivity
  • Planar Tunneling Spectroscopy
  • Quantum Materials
  • Thin Film Growth and Characterization
