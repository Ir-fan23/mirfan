About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Physics at Florida State University and a Graduate Research Assistant at the National High Magnetic Field Laboratory. My research focuses on strongly correlated electron systems, topological Kondo insulators, unconventional superconductivity, and planar tunneling spectroscopy.

Research Interests

  • Topological Kondo Insulators (SmB₆ and YbB₁₂)
  • Heavy Fermion Superconductivity
  • Planar Tunneling Spectroscopy
  • Quantum Materials
  • Thin Film Growth and Characterization
