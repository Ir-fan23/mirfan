Research

Topological Kondo Insulators

My research focuses on understanding the interaction between topological surface states and bulk excitations in candidate topological Kondo insulators including SmB₆ and YbB₁₂.

Using planar tunneling spectroscopy and harmonic detection techniques, I investigate low-energy electronic states and emergent many-body phenomena in these strongly correlated systems.

Heavy Fermion Superconductivity

I develop planar tunnel junctions to probe Cooper pair formation and unconventional superconductivity in heavy fermion materials, particularly the CeCoIn₅ family.

Thin Film Quantum Materials

Development of superconducting thin films, tunnel barriers, heterostructures, and quantum devices using sputtering, thermal evaporation, and vacuum-based fabrication techniques.
