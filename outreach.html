Outreach Philosophy

I strongly believe that scientific research should extend beyond the laboratory. Through outreach, mentorship, and public engagement, I strive to make physics accessible to broader audiences while helping students discover opportunities in science, research, and higher education. These activities complement my research by fostering the next generation of scientists and strengthening connections between academia and society.
